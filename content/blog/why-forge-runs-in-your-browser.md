Forge is not “another upload site.”

The product thesis is simple: **finish the job on your machine**. That means formatters, converters, and (soon) PDF/image tools run with browser APIs and local libraries — not a mystery server queue.

## What you get

- No account wall for MVP tools
- No “your file is being uploaded” anxiety for local tools
- A site that can stay on Vercel Free because we are not renting GPUs or storage for your documents

## What we will not pretend

Some jobs are hard entirely in-browser (huge video, OCR farms). Those stay off the roadmap until we can ship them honestly.

Building in public starts with foundations — then one phase a day.
