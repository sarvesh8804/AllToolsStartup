Photos often embed more than pixels: camera model, timestamps, and sometimes GPS coordinates. That is useful for photographers — and risky when you post a home exterior or a child’s school event.

## Strip metadata locally

Forge [EXIF Remover](/image/exif-remover) re-encodes the image in the browser so common metadata containers are dropped before download. Your original file never needs to leave the device.

## Pair with other image tools

- [Image Resizer](/image/image-resizer) — shrink before email or forms
- [Image Compressor](/image/image-compressor) — smaller downloads
- [WebP Converter](/image/webp-converter) — web-ready formats
- [PNG to JPG](/image/png-to-jpg) / [JPG to PNG](/image/jpg-to-png)

## Habit worth keeping

Before sharing a sensitive photo: remove EXIF, then resize. Two minutes prevents years of accidental location leakage.
